Need to message a delivery person, a customer or a shop just once? Don't fill your contacts with numbers you'll never use again.

Type or paste the number, add a message if you like, and open the chat directly in WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business.

WHAT IT DOES
• Chat with any number without saving it to contacts
• Choose WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business (whichever is installed)
• Smart number cleanup: +91 98765-43210, 09876543210, 0091… all work
• Paste: picks the phone number out of any copied text
• Share an SMS or note to the app and it finds the number
• Select a number in any app and choose this app from the copy/paste menu
• Ready message templates in Hindi and English
• Recent numbers list, kept only on your phone
• Share a chat link with anyone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No permissions at all. No contacts, no call log, no internet permission
• Nothing is uploaded. Recent numbers stay on your phone and you can delete them anytime
• Tiny app, under 100 KB

The message is only pre-filled. You always tap send yourself.

Available in English and Hindi.
